---
title: Volumes
description: Keep data around between deployments.
---

A service's filesystem is thrown away on every deployment. Anything your app writes to disk and needs later belongs on a volume: uploads, SQLite files, caches you want to keep.

Databases you create through Unbind manage their own storage. You do not add volumes to them.

## Create a volume

Open the command panel and pick **Volume**. Choose the service to mount it on, then the path where it should appear inside the service. The default is `/data`.

Press **Deploy** on the changes bar. The volume is created and the service restarts with it mounted.

- **One volume per service.**
- **The path has to match your app.** If your app writes uploads to `/app/uploads`, mount the volume there.

## Size

A new volume starts small. Open it to see **Usage**: how much is used out of the total. Unbind warns you at 75% and again at 85%.

To grow it, use **Expand**, pick the new size and confirm. It takes a couple of minutes and the data stays in place.

<Callout type="warn" title="Volumes only grow">
  A volume cannot be made smaller. Start small and expand when you need to.
</Callout>

## Replicas

A volume can be mounted on one replica at a time. A service with a volume should run a single [replica](/services/replicas-and-health), and during a deployment the old version stops before the new one starts, so expect a short gap.

If you need several replicas, keep the state somewhere shared instead: a database, or [S3 compatible storage](/templates/minio).

## Change the mount path

Open the volume and edit the **Mount Path** under **Connection**. Press **Deploy** on the changes bar and the service restarts with the volume at the new path. The data stays in place, only the path your app sees changes.

The reset button next to the field brings back the current path.

A database volume has no path to edit. The database mounts it where its engine expects.

## Unmount

Open the volume and press **Unmount** next to the service under **Mounted On**, then press **Deploy** on the changes bar. The service restarts without the volume, and the data stays in the volume.

The volume shows as unmounting until the old version of the service stops. After that it waits in the environment, unmounted. Open it and pick a service under **Mount to Service** to mount it again, on the same service or another one.

A database volume cannot be unmounted. The database needs it to run.

## Delete

- **Deleting a service keeps its volume.** The volume stays in the environment, unmounted, and can be mounted on another service. Open it and pick a service under **Mount to Service**, then set the **Mount Path**.
- **Deleting an environment or a project deletes its volumes**, with everything in them.
- **A volume can be deleted only when it is not mounted.** [Unmount](#unmount) it first, then open it and use **Delete Volume**. This cannot be undone.

## Backups

Volumes are not backed up by Unbind. [Database backups](/services/databases#backups) cover databases only. If the files matter, copy them somewhere else on a schedule.
