title: Service Groups
description: Keep services that belong together in one group.
---

A service group is a labelled box around services that belong together. It has a name, an icon and a description, and it keeps an environment readable once it holds more than a handful of services.

## Where groups come from

Deploying a [template](/templates) creates a group with all of the template's services inside. Supabase arrives as one group of nine services, not nine loose cards.

Creating a group by hand, or moving services in and out of one, is not in the UI yet. Both are available through the [API and the MCP server](/api).

## Ordering

The services page searches and sorts the same way as the [projects page](/teams/projects-and-environments#finding-and-ordering-projects). Searching for a group's name shows the whole group.

On **Custom**, drag a group to move it with all of its services, or drag a service inside a group to reorder the group. Services stay in their group while you drag: one dropped outside its group goes back where it was. The order is shared by everyone who can see the environment, and changing it needs the editor role. New services and groups go to the top, and a service added to a group goes to the top of that group.

Through the API, `list-service` takes the same `sort_by` and `sort_order` as `list-projects`. `reorder-services` orders the groups and the services outside them, and `reorder-service-group` orders the services inside one group.

## Rename

Open the menu on the group and choose **Rename** to change its name and description.

## Delete

**Delete** on a group deletes **every service inside it**. This cannot be undone.

The [volumes](/services/volumes) of those services are kept. They stay in the environment, unmounted, until you delete them or mount them somewhere else.

Deleting the last service of a group removes the group as well.

## Template settings

A group that came from a template remembers what you filled in when you deployed it. Domains, storage sizes and other visible inputs can be changed later through the API, and the template updates every setting that depends on them. Storage can only grow.
