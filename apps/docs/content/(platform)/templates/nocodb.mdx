[NocoDB](https://nocodb.com) puts a spreadsheet interface on top of a database. It is often used as an open-source Airtable alternative.

Deploy it from the [template list](/templates#deploy-a-template). It takes a minute or two.

## What gets deployed

<TemplateServices name="NocoDB" />

Attachments are kept on a volume mounted at `/usr/app/data/`. Tables and records live in the PostgreSQL service.
