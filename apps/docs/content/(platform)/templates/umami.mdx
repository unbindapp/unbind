[Umami](https://umami.is) is simple, privacy focused web analytics. It is often used as a Google Analytics alternative.

Deploy it from the [template list](/templates#deploy-a-template). It takes a minute or two.

## What gets deployed

<TemplateServices name="Umami" />

Everything lives in the PostgreSQL service. The template creates no volume.

## After it deploys

Open your domain and sign in with Umami's default account:

- **Username**: `admin`
- **Password**: `umami`

<Callout type="warn" title="Change the password right away">
  Every Umami install starts with the same login. Change it under **Profile** before you do anything
  else.
</Callout>

Then add your website and put the tracking script Umami shows you on your pages.
