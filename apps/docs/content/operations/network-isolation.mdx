---
title: Network Isolation
description: Keep teams from reaching each other's services.
---

With team isolation on, a service only accepts connections from:

- Services in its own team
- Unbind itself: domains, builds, logs and metrics keep working
- Anywhere, on a port exposed on the servers with **Network Access**, since that port is public anyway
- The sources an exception allows

Everything else is refused, including a service in another team that calls the private address of yours. Outgoing connections are not limited: services still reach the internet and other teams' public domains.

It is on for new instances. Instances that existed before it start with it off, so you can add exceptions first.

## Turn it on

Go to **System** > **Settings** > **Network**. Only system admins see this page.

Before turning it on, find the services that call a service in another team and add an exception for each. Look for private addresses or `.svc.cluster.local` names of another team in variables and commands.

## Exceptions

An exception lets one service accept connections from a source outside its team:

- **A service in another team**, such as a server in one team calling a wallet in another. Only that service gets through, not the rest of its team.
- **Any source**, for a service that receives connections from servers or from hosts on your private network, such as callbacks from a node that runs outside Unbind.

An exception opens every port of the service. Deleting a service removes its exceptions.

Services in the same team always reach each other, so an exception between them is refused.

## How it works

Unbind keeps a [NetworkPolicy](https://kubernetes.io/docs/concepts/services-networking/network-policies/) in every team namespace and one per exception, and checks them every minute. A policy changed or deleted by hand is put back. Team members can no longer edit NetworkPolicies in their namespace.

Your cluster's network plugin has to enforce NetworkPolicy. k3s does out of the box. On [your own Kubernetes](/operations/bring-your-own-kubernetes), check your plugin.
